Our Story

TorqMax was founded with a simple conviction — Indian car owners deserve floor mats that actually fit their cars, survive their roads, and look great doing it. What started as a small workshop in Surat has grown into a brand trusted by 1,200+ drivers across India.

Every mat we make goes through a 12-step quality check before it ships. We use OEM-grade materials, 3D-scanned templates, and a zero-tolerance approach to fit.

How to Install the Android App

1

Download the APK

Tap the orange "Download for Android" button above to download the TorqMax-Android.apk file.

2

Allow Unknown Sources

Go to Settings → Security and enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

3

Open the APK File

Open your Downloads folder, tap TorqMax-Android.apk, and tap Install.

4

Launch TorqMax

Find the TorqMax icon on your home screen or app drawer and start configuring your custom mats!
